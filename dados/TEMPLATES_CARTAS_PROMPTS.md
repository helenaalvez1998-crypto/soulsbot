# Prompts dos templates completos das cartas (5 raridades)

Como usar: anexe `guia_template_carta_limpo.png` na IA de imagem e cole **um** dos prompts abaixo. Cada prompt já é completo (layout + estilo da raridade) e a intensidade visual sobe de 1 (Comum) a 5 (Primordial). Gere em retrato **840 × 1240** (ou o mais próximo que a ferramenta permitir; até ~6% de diferença de proporção é aceito). Se aparecer qualquer letra, apague no editor antes de enviar em `/adm` → Jogos, Coliseu e runs → Templates das cartas.

Dicas: gere as cinco na mesma ferramenta e com o mesmo guia. Se a IA deslocar uma placa, não precisa refazer: o bot detecta a janela da arte sozinho e as demais posições podem ser ajustadas com um arquivo JSON (veja `TEMPLATES_CARTAS_GUIA.md`). Se uma raridade baixa sair chamativa demais, repita o pedido acrescentando: "less glow, fewer ornaments, more muted".


## Comum

```
Using the attached layout guide image as the exact reference for where things go, create the TEMPLATE of a vertical collectible trading card, portrait 840x1240 px, fully opaque (no transparent areas, no rounded-corner cutouts). The template is EMPTY: do NOT draw any text, letters, numbers, logos, watermarks, characters or scenery anywhere.

Layout (follow the guide, same positions on every card):
- Top right: a small empty badge plate for the rarity name.
- Below it: a wide empty name banner plate, room for two lines of text.
- Center: one large horizontal ART WINDOW (about 87% of the card width, ~5:4), filled with FLAT PURE MAGENTA #FF00FF, no gradient, no shadow, no texture, no glow spilling into it, crisp straight edges.
- Lower left: an empty round medallion for a big power number.
- Lower right: two stacked, empty horizontal plates (type and cost).
- Bottom: a wide empty rectangular plate for ability text.

Readability rule for every rarity: the inside of every plate and medallion stays calm, dark and low-contrast so white text is readable. All the spectacle (glow, particles, light rays, ornaments, background effects) lives ONLY on the outer frame, the card margins and the background around the plates, never inside the plates or the art window. Dark-fantasy souls-like mood, symmetric and centered.

FLASHINESS LEVEL: 1 of 5 (the plainest card of the whole set).
RARITY STYLE (COMMON): a humble, worn, matte frame of dark iron and dull brown-gray steel, flat lighting, no glow at all, no gems, no particles. Thin plain border, four small rivets at the corners, simple beveled plates with light scratches and rust stains, flat dark charcoal background with a faint paper-like grain. Everything is muted and desaturated; the most saturated color on the card is a dull silver. Looks like cheap, old equipment.

Final check: no text anywhere, window is flat magenta #FF00FF with a clean edge, all plates empty and dark inside, layout identical to the guide, and the spectacle matches the flashiness level stated above (do not make a lower-level card as flashy as a higher one).
```
